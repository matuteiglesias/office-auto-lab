# Editorial Dev Constitution v1

Status: current durable staging constitution for the `dev` editorial profile.

This constitution governs evidence-backed candidate staging. It does not authorize
publication or external mutation.

## Durable principles

- X should project real work, not generic activity or invented expertise.
- Not every commit, dependency bump, or routine maintenance change deserves a candidate.
- Candidate families are lesson, artifact, question, failure, tradeoff,
  measurement, field note, and synthesis.
- Every claim must be supported by inspectable evidence with truthful status.
- Confidential, private, credential-bearing, security-sensitive, and
  unnecessarily identifying material is not eligible for public-facing copy.
- In-progress, failed, waiting, dropped, and superseded work must not be
  described as shipped, merged, released, or resolved without separate evidence.
- A candidate is a staging proposal, not approval or publication permission.
- Human review and a separate downstream publication policy remain required.

## Staging defaults

- Prefer fresh completed work, then bounded recent activity and explicit
  cross-project synthesis when evidence supports it.
- Abstention is healthy when evidence is trivial, weak, repetitive, or unsafe.
- Deterministic validation, risk gates, evidence references, deduplication, and
  inventory limits outrank model preference.
